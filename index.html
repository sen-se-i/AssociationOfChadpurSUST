About the Association

Rooted in Chandpur,
Thriving at SUST

The Association of Chandpur at Shahjalal University of Science and Technology (SUST), Sylhet, is a vibrant community of students hailing from the culturally rich district of Chandpur, Bangladesh. Founded to foster solidarity and belonging, we welcome every Chandpur student who walks through SUST's gates.

Far from home, we become each other's family — sharing stories of the Padma , Meghna and Dakatia rivers, the taste of fresh Hilsha, and the warmth of Chandpur's festivals. We bridge homesickness with brotherhood, turning a university corridor into a piece of Chandpur.

Beyond bonding, we represent Chandpur's voice across SUST's diverse association landscape — collaborating, contributing, and celebrating our heritage with pride.

The Land We Come From

The Rich Legacy of Chandpur

Chandpur is no ordinary district. Nestled at the confluence of the mighty Meghna, Dakatia and Padma rivers, it is a land of extraordinary natural beauty, deep cultural heritage, and the crown jewel of Bangladesh's fisheries — the Ilish (Hilsha).

Why We Exist
